# Background checks

Status: NOT IMPLEMENTED

Questionnaire answer: **NO.** ReFi does not perform pre-employment or
periodic background checks on personnel, contractors, or founders. No
screening provider is engaged, no consent process exists, and no records
exist. Do not answer "in progress" or "planned" unless one of those becomes
true and is recorded here.

## Policy statement (adopted 2026-09-10; not yet carried out)

- Before a person is granted access to RESTRICTED data or to production
  systems, ReFi intends to conduct a background check appropriate to the role
  and permitted by applicable law (identity verification, criminal record
  where lawful, and — for roles handling investor funds or advice —
  regulatory disqualification screening).
- Checks are performed by a third-party provider with the person's written
  consent; results are held by the owner only, are not stored in the
  repository, and are retained only as long as the person holds the role.
- Contractors and vendors' staff are covered by the vendor's own screening
  commitments in the contract, not by ReFi checks.
